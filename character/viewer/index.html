How the wardrobe works

  • baseOne continuous body, neck to toes, rigged with automatic bone-heat weights and split into 9 hideable parts: neck, torso, upper arms, forearms, hands, pelvis, thighs, shins, feet.
  • fitEach garment is built from the body's skeleton with ease added, cut to length, then pushed 6 mm outside the skin wherever it would sit inside.
  • bindGarment weights are copied from the nearest body surface: sleeves only from the arms, pants only from the pelvis and legs, so the fabric bends like the skin.
  • hideEach item lists the body parts it covers completely. The sweater hides the torso and upper arms, and the joggers hide the pelvis, thighs and shins.
  • faceHead modelled from the drawn profile. Eyes, brows, lashes, mouth and blush are one vector-drawn decal at the measured positions, and custom normals keep the face shading clean.
  • hairA continuous hair volume plus 64 tapered strand clumps from hairline to bun, a bun wound from thick clumps, face-framing locks and nape wisps. A strand texture adds edge shading and a highlight band.
  • nextSweater folds and bunched cuffs, fuller messy volume at the sides, and finer hands.

Using it in a game

  • parts/base_body.glb has the rig, body, head, hair, underwear and the Idle and Walk clips.
  • parts/sweater.glb, tshirt.glb, joggers.glb and shorts.glb each hold one item on the same 21-bone skeleton. In the engine, bind the item to the body's skeleton by bone name. That is Unity's shared-bones pattern, Unreal's Leader Pose component, or Godot's skeleton path.
  • wardrobe.json lists each item's slot and the body parts to hide while it is worn. The same data is on every glTF node as extras.
  • character.glb bundles everything, which is what this viewer loads. Units are meters, Y-up, facing +Z.

How to steer the next iteration

Leave a comment on this page or tell me in chat. Specific notes turn into parameter changes fastest, for example:

  • "Head 10% smaller, neck shorter, a wider scoop neckline."
  • "Sweater looser and longer, covering the waistband."
  • "Bun bigger and rounder, fewer loose strands."
  • "Add a hoodie and fuzzy socks to the wardrobe."
  • "Target Unity, 20k triangles, Mixamo-compatible bone names."
